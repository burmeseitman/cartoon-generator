# Cartoon Style Guide

This document captures the visual style, color palette, and design rules for generating cartoons. The environment and characters adapt to the news topic, but the core art style and color tone stay consistent.

---

## 1. Core Art Style (Always Fixed)

- **Warm cartoon illustration** — approachable, not dark or satirical
- **Soft watercolor-like coloring** with gentle gradients, no harsh black outlines
- **Cute, rounded character designs** — big eyes, simple facial features, friendly expressions
- **Color palette**: warm beige/cream tones (#f5e6c8, #edd9b0), brown wood tones, soft pastel clothing colors
- **Flat shading** with subtle gradient transitions, no dramatic lighting
- **Speech bubbles**: white rounded ovals with thin black borders
- **Yellow title banner** at the very top with bold black Burmese text
- **Artist signature** in bottom-right corner (small curved mark)

---

## 2. Character Design Rules (Always Fixed)

### General Character Traits
- **Big expressive eyes** — round, wide-set, always showing emotion clearly
- **Simple rounded heads** — no sharp angles, soft chin lines
- **Minimal facial details** — small nose dots, simple mouth shapes
- **Body proportions**: slightly oversized heads, compact bodies (chibi-ish but not extreme)
- **Clothing**: casual, colorful patterns (plaid, floral, striped)
- **Skin tone**: warm light-brown / peach

### Human Characters — Burmese People
- **Hair**: black, styled naturally (buns, short cuts, tied back)
- **Traditional/casual wear**: longyi (sarong-style wrap), casual shirts, slippers
- **Facial features**: warm smiles, rounded faces, expressive eyebrows
- **Posture**: relaxed, approachable, often sitting or standing in casual poses

### Animal Characters — Don't Care Attitude
- **Facial expression**: deadpan, unimpressed, sleepy, or completely indifferent — the animal is completely unbothered by the chaos around it
- **Posture**: any random or lazy posture — sprawled out, sitting oddly, staring into space, licking a paw, or just existing in the background
- **Contrast**: the more chaotic or dramatic the news is, the more casually the animal reacts (or doesn't react at all)
- **Placement**: anywhere in the scene — foreground, background, on furniture, upside down — the animal just does its own thing
- Examples: cat cleaning itself during a data breach, dog sleeping through a robot uprising, bird preening on a windowsill during a hacking scandal

---

## 3. Environment (Adapts to News Topic)

The environment changes based on the news story. Pick a setting that fits the topic:

- **Office / Co-working space** — desks, computers, whiteboards (for workplace or business news)
- **Hospital / Clinic** — waiting room, examination bed, medical screens (for healthcare news)
- **Classroom / School** — desks, chalkboard, books (for education news)
- **Living room / Home** — sofa, coffee table, TV (for general tech news)
- **Outdoor park / Street** — bench, trees, street lights (for social or environmental news)
- **Server room / Data center** — racks of servers, blinking lights (for cybersecurity news)
- **Any setting that matches the news context** — be creative but keep it simple

### Background Elements (Adapt Per Scene)
Include 3-5 background props relevant to the setting:
- Office: monitor, coffee mug, sticky notes, filing cabinet
- Hospital: stethoscope, clipboard, medicine bottles, waiting chairs
- School: books, backpack, globe, pencils
- Home: potted plant, calendar, teacup, laptop
- Server room: server racks, cables, cooling fans, status lights

Always keep the background simple — no complex architectural detail.

---

## 4. Characters (Adapt to News Topic)

Instead of the same family every time, create characters that fit the news context:

- **Office workers** — for business or workplace news
- **Doctors / Nurses / Patients** — for healthcare news
- **Students / Teachers** — for education news
- **Hackers / IT staff** — for cybersecurity news (stereotypical hoodie-and-glasses or uniformed IT)
- **Scientists / Researchers** — for AI or science news
- **General public** — for social or everyday news
- **A robot or AI character** — optional, for tech-related news (white dome head, blue LED eyes, friendly)

### Character Count
- 2-5 characters per scene
- At least one character should show a strong emotional reaction to the news (shock, laughter, confusion)
- One character can be completely unfazed (especially an animal)

---

## 6. Speech Bubble Rules

- **Shape**: Rounded white ovals with thin black stroke (~2px)
- **Tail**: Points toward the speaking character's mouth
- **Text**: Bold black Burmese font, centered inside bubble
- **Placement**: Above or beside the speaker's head
- **Quantity**: 2-5 speech bubbles per scene
- **Content**: Humorous Burmese dialogue related to the news topic

---

## 7. Mood & Tone Guidelines

- **Humorous but wholesome** — funny situation, not mean-spirited
- **Light satire** on the news topic — not political or aggressive
- **At least one character reacts dramatically** to the news (shock, laughter, facepalm)
- **At least one animal is completely unbothered** — adds comedic contrast
- **No anger or conflict** between characters — the humor comes from the situation
- **Warm, inviting atmosphere** regardless of the setting

---

## 8. Prompt Template

Use this structure when generating new cartoons:

```
A warm cartoon illustration in a [ENVIRONMENT]. 
[Character descriptions relevant to the news topic — e.g. office workers, doctors, students]
[Animal character with a "don't care" expression doing something random]
[Scene: what's happening, what's funny about the news topic]
[Speech bubbles with humorous Burmese dialogue]
Yellow title banner at top with bold black Burmese text saying [TOPIC IN BURMESE].
Soft watercolor-like coloring, gentle gradients, cute rounded character designs.
Big expressive eyes, friendly smiles.
[Background props relevant to the setting]
Artist signature in bottom-right corner.
```

---

## 9. What NOT To Do

- ❌ No harsh black outlines or comic-book style
- ❌ No dark/gritty backgrounds or dramatic shadows
- ❌ No aggressive or angry character expressions
- ❌ No realistic photo style or 3D rendering
- ❌ No political caricature or mean-spirited humor
- ❌ No complex architectural backgrounds — keep it simple
- ❌ No heavy saturated neon colors — stick to warm pastels
- ❌ Don't reuse the same family in every cartoon — adapt characters to the news
- ❌ Don't make animals overly expressive — they should be indifferent or mildly curious at most
