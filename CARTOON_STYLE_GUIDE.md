# Cartoon Style Guide

This document captures the visual style, character design, and composition rules from the reference cartoon. Use this guide for every cartoon generation to maintain consistency.

---

## 1. Art Style

- **Warm family comic illustration** — cozy, approachable, not dark or satirical
- **Soft watercolor-like coloring** with gentle gradients, no harsh black outlines
- **Cute, rounded character designs** — big eyes, simple facial features, friendly expressions
- **Lived-in home interior setting** — wooden furniture, warm tones, everyday objects
- **Color palette**: warm beige walls (#f5e6c8), brown wood tones, soft pastel clothing colors
- **Flat shading** with subtle gradient transitions, no dramatic lighting
- **Speech bubbles**: white rounded ovals with thin black borders
- **Yellow title banner** at the very top with bold black text
- **Artist signature** in bottom-right corner (small curved mark)

---

## 2. Character Design Rules

### General Character Traits
- **Big expressive eyes** — round, wide-set, always showing emotion clearly
- **Simple rounded heads** — no sharp angles, soft chin lines
- **Minimal facial details** — small nose dots, simple mouth shapes
- **Body proportions**: slightly oversized heads, compact bodies (chibi-ish but not extreme)
- **Clothing**: casual home wear, colorful patterns (plaid, floral, striped)
- **Skin tone**: warm light-brown / peach for all human characters

### Character Archetypes (Recurring)

#### The Father Figure
- Black hair tied in a small bun or topknot
- White button-up shirt
- Green plaid pants (longyi/traditional wrap style)
- Sits on a wooden chair or stool
- Often reading a newspaper or magazine
- Big open smile, wide-eyed expression

#### The Mother Figure
- Black hair with a flower accessory (yellow/white)
- Pink or coral long-sleeve top
- Purple patterned skirt/longyi
- Sits on the floor or low cushion
- Holds a teacup or cooking utensil
- Warm, caring smile

#### The Child/Son
- Short neat black hair
- Bright yellow t-shirt
- Purple or blue patterned pants
- Sits cross-legged on the floor
- Expressive reactions: worried, curious, surprised, thoughtful
- Often resting chin on hand

#### The Robot/AI Character
- White dome-shaped head with rounded edges
- Blue glowing LED eyes (teal/cyan color)
- Smiling mouth (simple curved line)
- Body features a tech/AI company logo on chest
- Flexible articulated arms (one may be a hose/tube type)
- Friendly and helpful posture — never threatening
- Sitting at a table with a laptop

#### The Pet Cat
- Orange tabby stripes
- Sleeping curled up on the floor
- "Z z z" floating above head
- Adds warmth and coziness to the scene

---

## 3. Scene Composition

### Layout Structure
- **Title banner** spans the full width at the very top (yellow rectangle, bold black text)
- **Characters arranged around 1-2 low round wooden coffee tables**
- **Two-tier seating**: some on chairs, some on floor cushions
- **Central focal character** (usually the robot/AI) sits at the center table
- **Surrounding characters** positioned left, right, and bottom corners
- **Background wall** takes up upper portion of the frame

### Background Elements (Always Include)
- **Calendar** hanging on the wall (with numbers/dates visible)
- **Potted plant** on a side table or shelf
- **Teapot and cups** on a tray somewhere in the scene
- **Cookies or snacks** on a plate on the coffee table
- **Laptop computer** on the central table
- **Sleeping cat** on the floor (orange tabby, with "Z z z")
- **Wall poster or framed picture** (optional, can include a small cartoon face)

### Spatial Arrangement
```
    [YELLOW TITLE BANNER - full width]
    
    [wall]  [calendar]        [poster]  [wall]
    
    Father          Robot          Mother
    (chair)      (at table)      (floor cushion)
    
                 [coffee table]
            cookies, laptop, teacups
    
    Son (floor, bottom-left)
```

## 5. Speech Bubble Rules

- **Shape**: Rounded white ovals with thin black stroke (~2px)
- **Tail**: Points toward the speaking character's mouth
- **Text**: Bold black font, centered inside bubble
- **Placement**: Above or beside the speaker's head
- **Language**: Can be any language (the reference uses Burmese)
- **Quantity**: 2-5 speech bubbles per scene
- **Content**: Humorous dialogue related to the news topic

---

## 6. Mood & Tone Guidelines

- **Humorous but wholesome** — funny situation, happy characters
- **Light satire on technology/AI** — AI portrayed as helpful, friendly, part of the family
- **No conflict or anger** — even when discussing serious news, the tone stays warm
- **Family bonding atmosphere** — everyone engaged, smiling, or pleasantly surprised
- **Everyday domestic setting** — living room, coffee time, snack time

---

## 7. Prompt Template for Consistent Generation

Use this structure when generating new cartoons:

```
A warm family comic illustration in a cozy living room. 
[Character descriptions as specified above]
[Scene: what's happening, what's funny about it]
[Speech bubbles with dialogue in Burmese]
Yellow title banner at top with bold black text.
Warm beige walls, wooden furniture, potted plant, sleeping orange cat.
Soft watercolor-like coloring, gentle gradients, cute rounded character designs.
Big expressive eyes, friendly smiles, casual home clothing.
Artist signature in bottom-right corner.
```

---

## 8. What NOT To Do

- ❌ No harsh black outlines or comic-book style
- ❌ No dark/gritty backgrounds or dramatic shadows
- ❌ No aggressive or angry character expressions
- ❌ No realistic photo style or 3D rendering
- ❌ No political caricature or mean-spirited humor
- ❌ No complex architectural backgrounds — keep it a simple living room
- ❌ No text rendered inside the image itself (speech bubbles should have placeholder or simple text)
